# EU AI Act changes take effect on a new timeline

## What happened

Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, known as the Digital Omnibus on AI, amends the EU AI
Act. It entered into force on 27 July 2026.[^eu-regulation-2026-1744]
According to the European Parliament's summary, it:[^ep-summary]

- adds new prohibitions, applying from **2 December 2026**, on AI systems that
  produce non-consensual intimate images or child sexual abuse material;
- moves the main obligations for stand-alone high-risk systems (Annex III, such
  as hiring, credit scoring, and education tools) to **2 December 2027**;
- moves obligations for AI built into regulated products (Annex I) to
  **2 August 2028**;
- requires providers and deployers to support AI literacy among the people who
  operate their AI systems.

## Why it matters

Teams that build or use AI for people in the EU have a firm date for the new
prohibitions and more time, but not an exemption, for high-risk requirements.
The delay exists because standards and national authorities were not ready.

## What to do

- Check now that no product can generate the newly prohibited content, before
  2 December 2026.
- If you build or deploy AI in a high-risk area, use the extra time to prepare:
  document data sources, risk controls, and human oversight.
- Confirm exact obligations in the regulation text or with legal advice; this
  brief is a summary, not legal guidance.
