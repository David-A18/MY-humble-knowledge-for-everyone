# Federal Reserve minutes point to possible rate increases

## What happened

On 28 October 2026, the US Federal Reserve kept its policy interest rate (the
federal funds target range) at 3.75 to 4 percent. Ten members voted to hold;
two preferred a 0.25 percentage point increase.[^fomc-statement] The meeting
minutes, released on 18 November, report that almost all participants judged
that further increases would likely be appropriate to bring inflation back to
the 2 percent goal in time.[^fomc-minutes] The next scheduled decision is in
December.

## Why it matters

Interest rates affect technology work in practical ways. Higher rates make
borrowing and investment more expensive, which can slow hiring and new
projects. Companies also weigh rates when choosing between paying for cloud
capacity as they go and committing to multi-year discounts.

## What to do

No immediate action is needed. If you plan budgets or long-term cloud
commitments, revisit those plans after the December decision. The
[FinOps commitments guide](../../../finops/commitments-and-rate-optimization.md)
explains the trade-offs of committing to discounts.
